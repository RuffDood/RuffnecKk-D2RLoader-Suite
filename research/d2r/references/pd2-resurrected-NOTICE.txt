## Use

- You need your own legitimately owned copy of Diablo II: Resurrected. This project ships no Blizzard game files beyond the
  modified data described above.
- For offline / single-player use only. Do not use it on Battle.net: it can get your account actioned.
- Use at your own risk, with no warranty. Mods and plugins can crash the game and lose or corrupt characters and saves;
  back up yours.
